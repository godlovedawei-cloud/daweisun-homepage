03 / Research

Questions from
the bedside.

Three connected areas of basic, translational, and clinical research.

01

Brain protection & ischemic injury

Investigating cellular responses to ischemic injury, with a focus on autophagy, mitophagy, and neonatal hypoxic-ischemic brain injury. This work connects mechanistic neuroscience with questions in perioperative brain protection.

HIF-2α · Autophagy & mitophagy · Neuroprotection

02

Patient blood management

Clinical research in transfusion, oxygen delivery, and coagulation in surgical patients, including cardiac surgery. My work includes a prospective observational study of arterial-venous oxygen difference as a potential transfusion indicator.

Cardiac surgery · Transfusion · Perioperative coagulation

03

Perioperative clinical outcomes

Clinical trials and observational research addressing anesthetic strategies, postoperative cognition, and patient outcomes. Selected work includes randomized trials in endoscopic sedation and cognitive recovery after carotid endarterectomy.

Clinical trials · Postoperative cognition · Outcomes research
