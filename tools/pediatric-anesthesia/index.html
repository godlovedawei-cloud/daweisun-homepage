Fluids & Blood

Weight-based calculations
AUTO
Maintenance fluid
4-2-1 · cc/hr = mL/hr
—

1 cc = 1 mL. The hourly rates are equivalent.

First 10 kg: 4 cc/kg/hr
Next 10 kg: 2 cc/kg/hr
Above 20 kg: 1 cc/kg/hr

18 kg: 10×4 + 8×2 = 56 cc/hr
35 kg: 10×4 + 10×2 + 15×1 = 75 cc/hr

This estimates basal maintenance needs, not a fixed intraoperative order. Choose isotonic fluid, consider a balanced crystalloid per local policy, and individualize for surgical losses, perfusion, urine output and comorbidities. Do not automatically replace a full fasting deficit. Neonates and young infants may need glucose support and monitoring. Fluid reference.

Estimated blood volume
Age-specific mL/kg range
—
